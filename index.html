Leaf X-ray micro-CT Datasets

Leaf CT Hub is an open catalog for discovering and reusing plant X-ray micro-CT datasets. The hub provides preview images, segmentation labels, metadata, and download link to support leaf anatomy, phenotyping, and image segmentation research.

How It Works

Browse here, download from trusted repositories

Search, filter, and preview any dataset directly on this site. Each record links to its permanent, DOI-backed home on USDA Ag Data Commons for full-resolution downloads.

Have your own leaf X-ray micro-CT dataset? Submit it to be added to the catalog.
